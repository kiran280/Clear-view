STILL HAVE A QUESTION? Talk to Our Team ↗
01 Do I need an appointment for an eye examination?

Booking an appointment is recommended to help us arrange a convenient time for your visit. Contact our team to enquire about availability.

02 How often should I have my eyes tested?

The recommended frequency depends on your age, vision needs and eye health. Your eye-care professional can suggest an appropriate schedule.

03 Can I get help choosing the right frames?

Absolutely. Our team can help you explore different frame shapes, styles and fits to find an option that suits your preferences and needs.

04 Do you offer prescription sunglasses?

Ask our team about available prescription sunglasses, lens options and suitable frame styles.

05 Can I request adjustments to my existing frames?

Contact or visit our store to enquire about frame fitting and adjustment services. Our team can advise you based on your frames.

06 How long does it take to receive my glasses?

Delivery or collection time depends on your prescription, selected lenses and frame availability. Our team can provide an estimated timeline when you place your order.

07 What should I bring to my eye examination?

Bring your current glasses or contact lens details, any previous prescription and relevant eye-care information you would like to discuss.

08 How can I contact Clear View for assistance?

You can reach us by phone, email or through the contact form on this page. You are also welcome to visit our store during opening hours.
