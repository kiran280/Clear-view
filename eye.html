INSIDE YOUR EYE EXAMINATION

What We
Evaluate.

Your eyes deserve more than a quick glance. Our eye examination may include a series of important assessments to understand your vision and monitor your overall eye health.

✓

Visual Acuity

How clearly you see objects at different distances.

✓

Prescription Assessment

Checking for refractive errors such as myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism.

✓

Eye Pressure

Measuring intraocular pressure when clinically indicated.

✓

Eye Coordination

Assessing how well both eyes work together and focus.

✓

Overall Eye Health

Examining relevant eye structures for signs of potential concerns.

Bring Your Current Glasses
